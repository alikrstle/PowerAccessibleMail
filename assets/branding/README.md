## Logo

The mark combines a blue envelope with two amber sound-wave arcs. It represents
email access through spoken and assistive navigation. Keep its proportions and
colors unchanged, and do not place text inside the square application icon.

- Blue: `#1756A9`
- Amber: `#F4B942`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Arabic accessible description: `شعار Power Accessible Mail، ظرف بريد أزرق بجواره موجتان صوتيتان ذهبيتان.`
- English accessible description: `Power Accessible Mail logo, a blue envelope beside two amber sound waves.`

## Generation record

The logo was generated with the built-in image generation tool as a flat,
vector-friendly software mark. The prompt requested a recognizable envelope and
outward sound-wave arcs, high contrast, no text or initials, no Gmail branding,
and clear rendering at 16 by 16 pixels. The generated chroma-key background was
removed locally and the transparent result was converted into the publishing,
OAuth, and Windows icon variants listed above.
