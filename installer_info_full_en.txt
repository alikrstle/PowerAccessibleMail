Privacy and ownership summary
- The application and its rights are owned by developer Ali Al-Amir.
- The application accesses email after OAuth consent or manual account configuration to provide the requested email features.
- Accounts and cached data are stored locally. Sensitive local data is protected with Windows DPAPI for the current Windows account.
- User data is not sold or used for advertising.
- When the user requests message translation, the selected message text is sent to Google Translate only to perform that request.
- The user can remove an account and its local cache from inside the application.
